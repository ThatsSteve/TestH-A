New ‘Ghirada’ apartment complex

Treviso, Italy

Location
Treviso, Italy
Client
Private customer
Typology
Residential / Interior
Size
7.000 mq
Status
In construction

The project involves the construction of two buildings for residential use.

The peculiarity of the intervention concerns their planimetric arrangement and the decreasing trend of the various levels to create an effect of volumetric variety and avoid introspection between the two buildings as much as possible. The particular section and the trend of the various levels have made it possible to develop large terraces used by the apartments as outdoor spaces with large plantings, thus making the liveability of the accommodation of the highest quality.
